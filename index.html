5.1 Blackbody Radiation - Origin of Quantum

Bullet Notes:
  • Blackbody α=1 perfect absorber/emitter, lab model small hole isothermal cavity
  • Stefan-Boltzmann 1879 j*=σT⁴ σ=5.67e-8 W/m²K⁴ total power, Wien 1893 λmaxT=b=2.898e-3 mK peak shift
  • Rayleigh-Jeans uν=8πν²/c³ kT fails high ν → UV catastrophe infinite energy
  • Planck 1900 E=nhν quantization solves, h=6.626e-34 Js Nobel 1918, birth quantum
  • Bhutan Example: Bukhari 800K λmax=3.6μm IR red glow, Sun 5800K 500nm green, Human 310K 9.3μm IR
DIAGRAM - Blackbody Curves (Simulation):
Wien: λmax=b/T, Stefan: P/A=σT⁴, Planck: Bλ=2hc²/λ⁵/(e^{hc/λkT}-1)
Derivation Step-by-Step: 1) Mode count in cavity g(ν)dν=8πν²/c³ dν (standing waves). 2) Classical equipartition ⟨E⟩=kT → uν=gkT = Rayleigh-Jeans. 3) ∫uν dν diverges. 4) Planck oscillator En=n hν Boltzmann P∝e^{-En/kT} → ⟨E⟩=hν/(e^{hν/kT}-1). 5) uν=g⟨E⟩ → Planck law. Limit hν<>kT → exponential cutoff finite.
SIMULATION - Wien Calculator: T= 5800K → λmax=500nm

2) Planck solved by? Quantization E=nhν Continuous

5.2 Photoelectric Effect

  • Hertz 1887 spark, Lenard 1902, Einstein 1905 photon E=hν Nobel 1921
  • Laws: No emission if ν<ν0 threshold metal, Kmax=hν-Φ ∝ ν not intensity, instant <1ns, photo-current ∝ intensity
  • Work function Φ=hν0 binding energy, Na 2.28eV Zn 4.3eV
  • Classical fails: should be time delay minutes, Kmax should depend intensity - both false
DIAGRAM - Photoelectric:
Kmax=hν-Φ, eV0=Kmax, ν0=Φ/h, hc=1240 eV·nm, photon momentum p=h/λ
Derivation: Photon absorbed by one electron. At surface hν=Φ+½mv²max. Deeper loses collisions → K
SIMULATION - Fire Photon: Metal Φ λnm

2) Intensity doubled current? Doubles Same

5.3 Compton Effect - Photon Momentum

  • Compton 1923 X-ray λ=0.071nm graphite scattered λ longer, shift depends θ not material
  • Classical Thomson no shift, photon particle p=h/λ elastic billiard collision proof
  • Δλ=λc(1-cosθ) λc=h/mec=0.002426nm very small need X-ray, visible shift tiny
  • Max shift 180° 0.00486nm
DIAGRAM - Compton Collision:
Δλ=(h/mec)(1-cosθ)=λc(1-cosθ), λc=0.002426nm
Derivation 5 Steps: 1) Initial photon (E=hc/λ p=h/λ) + electron rest (mec²,0). 2) Final photon (E' p' at θ) + recoil electron Ee=√(pe²c²+me²c⁴). 3) Conserve E+mec²=E'+Ee, p=p'cosθ+pex, 0=p'sinθ-pey. 4) pe²=p²+p'²-2pp'cosθ. 5) Square energy eq substitute → 1/E'-1/E=(1/mec²)(1-cosθ) → Δλ. Requires relativity.
SIMULATION: θ= 90° Δλ=0.002426nm
QUIZ 5.3: 1) Shift depends on? Angle Material
2) Why need X-ray? λc very small Visible energetic
3) Max at? 180° 0°

5.4 de Broglie Matter Wave

  • de Broglie 1924 PhD thesis if wave→particle then particle→wave λ=h/p symmetry
  • e- V=100V λ= h/√(2meV)=1.227/√V nm=0.123nm comparable atom spacing diffracts
  • Davisson-Germer 1927 Ni crystal 54eV peak at 50° proves wave, Bohr orbit 2πr=nλ → L=nħ
  • Electron microscope 100keV λ=0.0037nm vs optical 500nm 100000× better resolution
DIAGRAM - Matter Wave:
λ=h/p, e- λ(nm)=1.227/√V, Bohr standing wave 2πr=nλ
Derivation: From photon λ=h/p hypothesized universal. Standing wave condition circumference 2πr=nλ=nh/p → L=rp=nħ → En=-13.6/n²eV explains Bohr quantization.
SIMULATION: V= 100V λ=0.1227nm

2) 54eV λ? ~0.16nm 16nm

5.5 Wave-Particle Duality

  • Light duality: interference/diffraction wave, photoelectric/Compton particle → dual
  • Matter duality: electron diffraction wave, Millikan charge tracks particle
  • Bohr Complementarity 1928: Both aspects necessary but mutually exclusive same measurement
  • Quantum ψ wave amplitude, |ψ|² probability, correspondence S>>h classical
Critical Thinking: Human 70kg 1m/s λ=h/p=9.5e-36m << nuclear impossible detect. Radio 1m photon E=1.2μeV tiny huge number N→ classical wave emerges as average. Duality scale set by h.

5.7 Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle & Consequences

DIAGRAM - Confinement:
  • Heisenberg 1927 ΔxΔp≥ħ/2, ΔEΔt≥ħ/2, ħ=1.054e-34 Js, [x,p]=iħ non-commuting
  • Gamma-ray microscope thought experiment: Δx~λ small → Δp~h/λ large product ~h
  • Consequences: Zero-point energy E_min=ħ²/8mL² not zero, e- not in nucleus Δx=1e-14m → KE~20MeV, tunneling, natural linewidth, virtual particles, laser linewidth
ΔxΔp≥ħ/2, ΔEΔt≥ħ/2, ħ=h/2π
Derivation: Wave packet ψ(x)=∫A(k)e^{ikx}dk ΔxΔk≥½, p=ħk → ΔxΔp≥ħ/2. Formal (ΔA)²(ΔB)²≥|⟨[A,B]⟩|²/4 with [x,p]=iħ → ħ²/4. Energy-time similar Fourier t-ω.
SIMULATION: Confinement Δx= 0.5nm → Δv≥115 km/s
