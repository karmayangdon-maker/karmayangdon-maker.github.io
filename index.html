5.1 Blackbody Radiation

Bullet Notes:
  • Blackbody α=1 perfect absorber/emitter, lab small hole isothermal cavity - Kirchhoff 1859
  • Stefan-Boltzmann j*=σT⁴ σ=5.67e-8 total power, Wien λmaxT=b=2.898×10⁻³ mK peak shifts blue as T↑
  • Classical Rayleigh-Jeans uν=8πν²/c³ kT fails high ν → UV catastrophe infinite energy
  • Planck 1900 E=nhν quantization solves, h=6.626e-34 Js Nobel 1918 birth quantum
  • Bhutan Example: Bukhari 800K λmax=3.6μm IR red glow, Sun 5800K 500nm green, Human 310K 9.3μm IR
DIAGRAM - Blackbody Curves:
Wien λmax=b/T | Stefan P/A=σT⁴ | Planck Bλ=2hc²/λ⁵/(e^{hc/λkT}-1)
Derivation Step-by-Step: 1) Mode count g(ν)=8πν²/c³ dν. 2) Classical ⟨E⟩=kT → RJ uν=gkT. 3) ∫RJ diverges. 4) Planck En=n hν Boltzmann → ⟨E⟩=hν/(e^{hν/kT}-1). 5) uν=g⟨E⟩ → Planck law. Limit hν<>kT→exponential cutoff finite.
SIMULATION: T= 5800K λmax=500nm

2) Planck solved by? E=nhν Continuous

  • Laws: No emission if ν<ν0 threshold, Kmax=hν-Φ ∝ ν not intensity, instant <1ns, current ∝ intensity
  • Work function Φ=hν0 binding, Na 2.28eV Zn 4.3eV
  • Classical fails: should be delay minutes, Kmax should depend intensity

5.3 Compton Effect

  • Compton 1923 X-ray 0.071nm graphite scattered λ longer shift depends θ not material
  • Classical Thomson no shift, photon particle p=h/λ elastic collision proof
  • Δλ=λc(1-cosθ) λc=0.002426nm very small need X-ray
DIAGRAM:
Δλ=h/mec(1-cosθ)=λc(1-cosθ) | Max 180° 0.00486nm
Derivation: Initial photon E=hc/λ p=h/λ + electron rest mec². Final photon E' p' at θ + electron Ee=√(pe²c²+me²c⁴). Conserve E+mec²=E'+Ee, p vector. pe²=p²+p'²-2pp'cosθ. Square energy eq → 1/E'-1/E=(1/mec²)(1-cosθ) → Δλ.
SIMULATION: θ= 90° Δλ=0.002426nm
QUIZ 5.3: 1) Shift depends on? Angle Material
2) Why X-ray? λc very small Visible too energetic
3) Max at? 180° 0°

5.4 de Broglie Matter Wave

  • e- V=100V λ=1.227/√V nm=0.123nm comparable atom spacing diffracts
  • Davisson-Germer 1927 Ni 54eV peak 50° proves wave, Bohr orbit 2πr=nλ → L=nħ
  • Electron microscope 100keV λ=0.0037nm vs optical 500nm 100000× better

5.5 Wave-Particle Duality

  • Light: interference wave, photoelectric/Compton particle → dual
  • Matter: diffraction wave, cloud chamber track particle

  • Quantum ψ wave, |ψ|² probability, correspondence S>>h classical

5.7 Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle

DIAGRAM:
  • Heisenberg 1927 ΔxΔp≥ħ/2, ΔEΔt≥ħ/2, [x,p]=iħ non-commuting
  • Gamma microscope: Δx~λ small → Δp~h/λ large product ~h
  • Consequences: Zero-point E_min=ħ²/8mL², e- not in nucleus Δx=1e-14m → KE~20MeV, tunneling, linewidth, virtual particles
ΔxΔp≥ħ/2 | ΔEΔt≥ħ/2 | ħ=1.054e-34 Js
