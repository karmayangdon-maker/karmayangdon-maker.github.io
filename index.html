5.1 Blackbody Radiation

Bullet Notes:
  • Blackbody α=1 perfect absorber/emitter
  • Classical Rayleigh-Jeans: \(u_ν=8πν²/c³ kT\) fails at high ν → UV catastrophe
  • Planck 1900: \(E_n=n hν\) quantization
  • Wien: Hotter → λmax blue shift, Stefan: Total power ∝T⁴
  • Sun 5800K → λmax~500nm, Human 310K → 9.3μm IR
Wien: λmaxT=2.898e-3 mK | Stefan: j*=σT⁴ | Planck: Bλ=2hc²/λ⁵ * 1/(e^hc/λkT-1)
Derivation: Mode density g(ν)=8πν²/c³. Classical =kT. Quantum =hν/(e^hν/kT-1) from Boltzmann P∝e^-nhν/kT. Multiply g*⟨E⟩ → Planck law. Low ν→RJ, high ν→exponential cut-off.

Example: Furnace 1500K → λmax=2898/1500=1.93μm IR.

5.2 Photoelectric Effect

  • Hertz 1887, Einstein 1905 photon E=hν
  • Facts: No emission if ν<ν0, KE_max ∝ frequency not intensity, instant <1ns, current ∝ intensity
  • Work function Φ metal binding
Kmax = hν - Φ | eV0=Kmax | ν0=Φ/h
Derivation: hν=Φ+Kmax at surface. Deeper electron loses more → K

Example: Φ=2.3eV, ν=7e14Hz → E=4.136e-15*7e14=2.9eV → Kmax=0.6eV.

5.3 Compton Effect

  • Compton 1923 X-ray graphite λ increased
  • Classical Thomson predicts no shift
  • Photon momentum p=h/λ, collision like billiard
  • λc=h/mec=0.002426nm
Δλ = (h/mec)(1-cosθ) | Max 180°: 0.00486nm
Derivation Steps: 1) Initial: photon E=hc/λ, electron rest mec² 2) Final: E'=hc/λ', pe, Ee=√(pe²c²+me²c⁴) 3) Cons: p=p'cosθ+pex, 0=p'sinθ-pey, E+mec²=E'+Ee 4) Eliminate pe → 1/E'-1/E=(1/mec²)(1-cosθ) → Δλ formula. Proves particle nature.

5.4 de Broglie Matter Wave

  • de Broglie 1924: if wave→particle, particle→wave λ=h/p
  • V=100V → λ≈0.123nm diffracts
  • Davisson-Germer 1927 Ni crystal proves
  • Bohr quantization: 2πr=nλ → pr=nħ
  • Electron microscope 0.1nm vs optical 500nm
λ=h/p = h/√(2meV) → e⁻: λ(nm)=1.227/√V
Derivation: λ=h/p, standing wave in orbit 2πr=nλ=nh/p → L=nħ → Bohr derived + energy -13.6/n²eV.

5.5 Wave-Particle Duality

  • Light: interference wave, photoelectric/Compton particle → dual
  • Matter: diffraction wave, Millikan particle
  • Bohr Complementarity: apparatus decides
  • ψ, |ψ|²=probability
Critical Thinking: Radio λ=1m photon E tiny → wave hidden (many photons). γ-ray λ=1e-12m particle dominates. Duality depends on h vs resolution → correspondence principle.

5.6 Electron Double-Slit

  • Young with electrons even one by one → fringe build-up
  • Each electron ψ_total=ψ1+ψ2, |ψ1+ψ2|² = |ψ1|²+|ψ2|²+interference
  • Detector which slit → interference vanishes
Maxima: d sinθ=mλ

5.7 Heisenberg Uncertainty

  • 1927 ΔxΔp≥ħ/2, ΔEΔt≥ħ/2
  • Gamma-ray microscope: Δx~λ, Δp~h/λ → ΔxΔp~h
  • Consequences: e⁻ not in nucleus (Δx~1e-14→KE~20MeV), zero-point E_min=ħ²/8mL², tunneling, laser linewidth
ΔxΔp≥ħ/2, ħ=1.054e-34 Js
Derivation: Fourier wave packet narrow in x → broad in k → broad in p. Formal via Schwarz inequality.
