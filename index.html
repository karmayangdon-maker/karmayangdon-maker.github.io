5.1 Blackbody Radiation

Bullet Notes:
  • Blackbody α=1 perfect absorber/emitter, laboratory small hole cavity
  • Kirchhoff 1859, Stefan 1879 j*=σT⁴, Wien 1893 λmaxT=2.898×10⁻³ mK
  • Classical Rayleigh-Jeans uν=8πν²/c³ kT fails at high ν → Ultraviolet Catastrophe
  • Planck 1900 revolutionary: E=n hν quantization, oscillators discrete
  • Sun 5800K → λmax≈500nm green, human body 310K → 9.3μm infrared
Wien: λmaxT=b, Stefan: j*=σT⁴, Planck: Bλ=2hc²/λ⁵ · 1/(e^hc/λkT-1)
Derivation: Mode density in cavity g(ν)=8πν²/c³. Classical equipartition ⟨E⟩=kT → RJ. Planck used Boltzmann P∝e^-nhν/kT → ⟨E⟩=hν/(e^hν/kT-1). Multiply g×⟨E⟩ gives Planck law. At hν<>kT → Wien exponential cut-off solves catastrophe.

Example: Furnace 1500K → λmax=2898/1500=1.93μm IR invisible, glows red only from tail.

5.2 Photoelectric Effect

  • Hertz 1887 spark, Lenard 1902, Einstein 1905 photon explanation Nobel 1921
  • Experimental facts: (i) No emission if ν<ν0 threshold (ii) Kmax∝ν not intensity (iii) Instant emission <1ns (iv) Current∝intensity
  • Work function Φ metal binding ~2-5eV
  • Classical wave fails: intensity should give energy time-delay
Kmax = hν - Φ , eV0 = Kmax , ν0=Φ/h , p_photon=h/λ
Derivation: Photon absorbed by one electron: hν=Φ+Kmax at surface. If electron from depth loses collision energy → K

Example: Na Φ=2.28eV, light 400nm → E=hc/λ=3.10eV → Kmax=0.82eV → V0=0.82V.

5.3 Compton Effect

  • Compton 1923 X-ray 0.071nm on graphite, scattered λ increased with angle
  • Classical Thomson scattering predicts no λ shift
  • Photon particle with momentum p=h/λ, collision like billiard balls
  • Compton wavelength λc=h/mec=0.002426nm very small → visible shift tiny
Δλ=λ'-λ=(h/mec)(1-cosθ) , λc=0.002426nm , Max at 180°=0.00486nm
Full Derivation: Initial: photon (E=hc/λ, p=h/λ) + electron rest (E0=mec², p=0). Final: photon (E'=hc/λ', p'=h/λ' at θ) + recoil electron Ee=√(pe²c²+me²c⁴). Conservation: p = p'cosθ+pex, 0=p'sinθ-pey, E+mec²=E'+Ee. Eliminate pe via pe²=pex²+pey² → (E+mec²-E')² = (p c)²+(p'c)²-2pp'c²cosθ+me²c⁴. Simplify → 1/E' -1/E = (1/mec²)(1-cosθ) → Δλ formula. Proves photon momentum, particle nature of light.

5.4 de Broglie Hypothesis

  • de Broglie 1924 PhD thesis: If wave→particle, then particle→wave λ=h/p
  • Electron V=100V → p=√(2meV) → λ=1.227/√V nm =0.123nm comparable to atoms → diffracts
  • Davisson-Germer 1927 Ni crystal 54eV peak at 50° proves wave
  • Bohr quantization derived: 2πr=nλ → pr=nħ → Ln=nħ
  • Electron microscope 100keV λ=0.0037nm vs optical 500nm → 100000× better resolution
λ=h/p , For e⁻: λ(nm)=1.227/√V , λc=h/√(2meV)
Derivation from Bohr: Standing wave condition circumference 2πr=nλ. With λ=h/p → 2πr=nh/p → L=rp=nħ. Energy En=p²/2m → En=-me⁴/2(4πε0)²ħ² ·1/n² = -13.6eV/n². de Broglie explains why quantization.

5.5 Wave-Particle Duality

  • Light: Interference/diffraction → wave, Photoelectric/Compton → particle → dual
  • Matter: Diffraction/interference → wave, Tracks in cloud chamber → particle
  • Bohr Complementarity: Measurement apparatus determines which aspect appears, both never together
  • Quantum: ψ wave function, |ψ|² probability density, not trajectory
Critical Thinking: When h negligible vs action S=p·x, particle picture works. When λ~d (aperture), wave dominates. Radio photon 1MHz E=4neV tiny many photons → classical wave emerges via correspondence. γ-ray single photon shows particle. Duality not contradiction but limitation of classical language.

5.6 Electron Double-Slit

  • Young 1801 light, Tonomura 1989 electrons one-by-one → buildup interference
  • Even single electron at a time → interference → self-interference ψ=ψ1+ψ2
  • Probability |ψ1+ψ2|²=|ψ1|²+|ψ2|²+2|ψ1||ψ2|cosδ interference term
  • Which-path detector → destroy coherence → interference disappears → complementarity
Bright: d sinθ=mλ , Dark: d sinθ=(m+½)λ

Demo: If we close one slit, pattern = single slit envelope. Open both, modulation fringes inside envelope. Quantum mystery: electron goes both paths as wave, hits screen as particle.

5.7 Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle

  • Heisenberg 1927 ΔxΔp≥ħ/2 , ΔEΔt≥ħ/2, Born from wave packet Fourier
  • Gamma-ray microscope thought experiment: To see electron Δx~λ, photon recoil Δp~h/λ → ΔxΔp~h
  • Consequences: (i) e⁻ not in nucleus: Δx=1e-14m → Δp~ħ/Δx → KE~20MeV > nuclear binding (ii) Zero-point energy: particle in box L → Δx~L → p≥ħ/2L → E_min=ħ²/8mL² not zero (iii) Tunneling, (iv) Laser linewidth ΔEΔt
  • Operator commutation [x,p]=iħ → cannot simultaneous eigenstate
ΔxΔp≥ħ/2=5.27e-35 Js , ΔEΔt≥ħ/2
Derivation Idea: Wave packet ψ(x)=∫A(k)e^ikx dk. Narrow Δx → broad Δk Fourier. With p=ħk → ΔxΔp≥ħ/2. Formal proof via Schwarz inequality ⟨(x-⟨x⟩)²⟩⟨(p-⟨p⟩)²⟩≥|⟨[x,p]⟩|²/4=ħ²/4.
