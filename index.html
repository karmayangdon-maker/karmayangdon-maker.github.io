5.1 Blackbody Radiation Origin of Quantum

Bullet Notes:
  • Blackbody α=1 perfect absorber and emitter, lab model: small hole in isothermal cavity
  • Kirchhoff 1859, Stefan-Boltzmann 1879: total emissive power j*=σT⁴, σ=5.67e-8 W/m²K⁴
  • Wien 1893: λmaxT=b=2.898×10⁻³ mK - hotter → bluer
  • Classical failure: Rayleigh-Jeans uν=8πν²/c³ kT → UV catastrophe as ν→∞, infinite energy
  • Planck 1900: Energy quantization E=nhν, h=6.626e-34 Js solved catastrophe, birth of quantum
  • Bhutan Example: Traditional bukhari at 800K glows red-orange λmax=3.6μm IR, Sun 5800K λmax=500nm
Wien: λmax= b/T | Stefan: P/A=σT⁴ | Planck: Bλ(T)= (2hc²/λ⁵) / (e^{hc/λkT}-1) | k=1.38e-23 J/K
Step-by-step Derivation:
1. Mode count in cavity: g(ν)dν=8πν²/c³ dν (standing waves).
2. Classical equipartition: each mode ⟨E⟩=kT → uν=g⟨E⟩=8πν²/c³ kT = Rayleigh-Jeans.
3. Problem: ∫0∞ uν dν diverges.
4. Planck: oscillator levels En=n hν, Boltzmann probability ∝e^{-En/kT}.
5. ⟨E⟩= Σ n hν e^{-n hν/kT} / Σ e^{-n hν/kT}= hν/(e^{hν/kT}-1).
6. uν=g⟨E⟩ → Planck law. Limit hν<>kT → Wien exponential cut-off, finite total energy.

5.2 Photoelectric Effect

  • Hertz 1887, Lenard 1902, Einstein 1905 photon E=hν Nobel 1921
  • Laws: (i) No emission if ν<ν0 threshold depends on metal (ii) Kmax = hν-Φ linear with ν not intensity (iii) Emission instant <10⁻⁹s (iv) Photo-current ∝ intensity
  • Work function Φ= hν0: Na 2.28eV, Zn 4.3eV
  • Classical wave: energy spread over wavefront, delay should be minutes, intensity should affect Kmax - both false
Kmax= hν - Φ | eV0=Kmax (stopping) | ν0=Φ/h | Photon momentum p=h/λ | hc=1240 eV·nm
Derivation: Conservation: photon absorbed by single electron. Energy at surface: hν=Φ+½mv²_max. Deeper electron suffers collisions → K< Kmax. Plot Kmax vs ν slope = h, intercept = -Φ. Millikan 1916 verified linearity, measured h accurately despite disliking photon idea.

5.3 Compton Effect

  • Compton 1923: X-ray λ=0.071nm on graphite, scattered λ longer, shift depends on θ, not material
  • Classical Thomson: electron oscillates at same ν, no shift
  • Proof of photon particle: p=h/λ, E=hc/λ, elastic collision with free electron
  • Compton shift Δλ=λc(1-cosθ), λc=h/mec=0.002426nm very small, need X-ray to see
Δλ = (h/m_e c)(1-cosθ)= λc(1-cosθ) | At 90° Δλ=λc | At 180° 2λc=0.00486nm
Derivation 5 Steps: 1) Initial 4-momentum: photon (E/c, p) + electron (mec,0). 2) Final: photon (E'/c, p' at θ) + electron (Ee/c, pe). 3) Conserve: Energy E+mec²=E'+Ee, Momentum vector p = p' + pe. 4) Write pe² = p²+p'²-2pp'cosθ, and Ee²=pe²c²+me²c⁴. 5) Square energy eq, substitute pe, cancel → 1/E' -1/E = (1/mec²)(1-cosθ) → hc/λ' vs hc/λ → Δλ result. Requires relativity.

5.4 de Broglie Matter Wave

  • de Broglie 1924 PhD: symmetry - if light wave can be particle, particle can be wave λ=h/p
  • For electron accelerated V: λ= h/√(2meV) =1.227/√V nm (non-relativistic)
  • Davisson-Germer 1927: 54eV electrons on Ni, Bragg peak at 50° matches λ=0.165nm → proof
  • Bohr model explained: orbit standing wave 2πr=nλ → angular momentum quantization
  • Application: Electron microscope, neutron diffraction, Bhutan hydropower turbine electron inspection
λ= h/p = h/mv | e⁻: λ(nm)=1.227/√V(volts) | Relativistic λ= h/√(2meV(1+eV/2mec²))
Derivation: Combine Planck E=hν and Einstein E=mc² for photon p=E/c=h/λ → λ=h/p hypothesis universal. For Bohr orbit: standing wave condition 2πr=nλ=nh/p → L=rp=nħ. Then En= L²/2mr² ... → -13.6/n² eV.

5.5 Wave-Particle Duality

  • Light duality: interference → wave, photoelectric/Compton → particle
  • Matter duality: electron diffraction → wave, Millikan charge → particle
  • Bohr Complementarity Principle 1928: Both aspects necessary but mutually exclusive in same measurement
  • Quantum: ψ wave amplitude, |ψ|² probability, not classical trajectory
  • Correspondence: S>>h → classical, S~h → quantum
Critical Thinking: Why we don't see duality daily? Human m=70kg v=1m/s → λ=h/p=9.5e-36m << nuclear size impossible to detect. For radio 1m wave, photon E=hν=1.24μeV tiny, huge number N~10²⁰ → classical wave emerges as average. Duality scale set by Planck constant h.

5.6 Electron Double-Slit Experiment

  • Young 1801 light, Jönsson 1961, Tonomura 1989 single electrons build interference
  • Even 1 electron at a time: each contributes dot, after 10000 dots → fringe pattern
  • Each electron ψ_total=ψ1+ψ2, probability P=|ψ1+ψ2|²=|ψ1|²+|ψ2|²+2Re(ψ1*ψ2) interference term
  • Which-path information destroys interference → measurement collapses wavefunction
  • Feynman: heart of quantum mystery

5.7 Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle

  • Heisenberg 1927 ΔxΔp≥ħ/2, ΔEΔt≥ħ/2, ħ=1.054e-34 Js
  • Thought experiment: gamma-ray microscope to locate electron: photon λ small → Δx~λ small but Compton recoil Δp~h/λ large → product ~h
  • Formal: [x,p]=iħ non-commuting operators → no simultaneous eigenstate, Fourier wave packet narrow in x → broad in k
  • Consequences: Zero-point energy, electron cannot be in nucleus, tunneling, natural linewidth, virtual particles
ΔxΔp_x ≥ ħ/2 | ΔyΔp_y ≥ ħ/2 | ΔEΔt ≥ ħ/2 | ħ=h/2π
Derivation Sketch: For wave packet ψ(x)=∫A(k)e^{ikx}dk, widths ΔxΔk≥½. Using de Broglie p=ħk → ΔxΔp≥ħ/2. Formal: For any operators A,B, (ΔA)²(ΔB)²≥|⟨[A,B]⟩|²/4. Plug [x,p]=iħ → ħ²/4. Energy-time from similar Fourier t-ω. Not measurement disturbance but intrinsic wave nature.
