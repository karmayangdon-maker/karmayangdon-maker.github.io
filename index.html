5.1 Blackbody Radiation

Bullet Notes:
  • Blackbody α=1 perfect absorber, lab model small hole isothermal cavity
  • Stefan j*=σT⁴ σ=5.67e-8 total power, Wien λmaxT=2.898e-3 mK peak shift blue as T↑
  • Rayleigh-Jeans uν=8πν²/c³ kT fails high ν → UV catastrophe infinite
  • Planck 1900 E=nhν quantization solves, h=6.626e-34 Js birth quantum
  • Bhutan Example: Bukhari 800K λmax=3.6μm IR red glow, Sun 5800K 500nm, Human 310K 9.3μm IR
DIAGRAM:

Blue 3000K, Red 5800K Sun, Orange 8000K. Peak moves left as T↑, area ∝T⁴. Dashed classical RJ blows up.

Wien λmax=b/T, Stefan j*=σT⁴, Planck Bλ=2hc²/λ⁵/(e^{hc/λkT}-1)
Derivation: Mode count g=8πν²/c³. Classical ⟨E⟩=kT → RJ divergent. Planck En=n hν Boltzmann → ⟨E⟩=hν/(e^{hν/kT}-1) → Planck law. hν<>kT→exponential cutoff finite.
SIMULATION: T= 5800K λmax=500nm

2) Planck solved by? E=nhν Continuous

5.2 Photoelectric Effect

  • Hertz 1887, Lenard 1902, Einstein 1905 photon E=hν Nobel 1921
  • Laws: No emission if ν<ν0, Kmax=hν-Φ ∝ ν not intensity, instant <1ns, current ∝ intensity
  • Work function Φ metal binding, Φ=hν0, Classical fails delay and Kmax vs intensity
DIAGRAM:
Kmax=hν-Φ, eV0=Kmax, ν0=Φ/h, hc=1240 eV·nm, p=h/λ
Derivation: Photon absorbed by one e-. At surface hν=Φ+Kmax. Deeper loses. Graph Kmax vs ν slope=h intercept=-Φ. Millikan 1916 verified.
SIMULATION: Φ λnm

5.3 Compton Effect

  • Compton 1923 X-ray 0.071nm graphite scattered λ longer shift depends θ not material
  • Classical Thomson no shift, photon particle p=h/λ elastic collision proof particle nature
  • Δλ=λc(1-cosθ) λc=0.002426nm small need X-ray
DIAGRAM:
Δλ=h/mec (1-cosθ)=λc(1-cosθ), Max 180° 0.00486nm
Derivation: Energy E+mec²=E'+Ee, momentum p=p'cosθ+pex, 0=p'sinθ-pey, pe²=p²+p'²-2pp'cosθ, Ee²=pe²c²+me²c⁴ → Square → 1/E'-1/E=(1/mec²)(1-cosθ) → Δλ.
SIMULATION: θ= 90° Δλ=0.002426nm
QUIZ 5.3: 1) Shift depends on? Angle Material
2) Why X-ray? λc very small Visible too energetic
3) Max at? 180° 0°

5.4 de Broglie Matter Wave

  • de Broglie 1924 if wave→particle then particle→wave λ=h/p
  • e- V=100V λ=0.123nm comparable atom spacing diffracts, V↑ λ↓
  • Davisson-Germer 1927 Ni 54eV peak 50° proof, Bohr orbit 2πr=nλ → L=nħ
  • Electron microscope 100keV λ=0.0037nm vs optical 500nm 100000× better
DIAGRAM:
λ=h/p, e- λ(nm)=1.227/√V, standing wave 2πr=nλ
Derivation: From photon λ=h/p universal. Bohr standing wave 2πr=nλ=nh/p → L=nħ → En=-13.6/n²eV explains quantization.
SIMULATION: V= 100V λ=0.1227nm

5.5 Wave-Particle Duality

  • Light: interference wave, photoelectric/Compton particle → dual
  • Matter: diffraction wave, cloud chamber track particle
  • Bohr Complementarity: apparatus decides which aspect, never both same time
  • Quantum ψ wave, |ψ|² probability, correspondence S>>h classical
Critical Thinking: Human 70kg 1m/s λ=h/p=9.5e-36m << nuclear impossible. Radio 1m photon E=1.2μeV huge N→ classical wave emerges. Duality scale set by h.

5.7 Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle

DIAGRAM:
  • Heisenberg 1927 ΔxΔp≥ħ/2, ΔEΔt≥ħ/2, [x,p]=iħ non-commuting
  • Gamma microscope: Δx~λ small → Δp~h/λ large product ~h
  • Consequences: Zero-point E_min=ħ²/8mL², e- not in nucleus (Δx=1e-14m → KE~20MeV), tunneling, linewidth, virtual particles
ΔxΔp≥ħ/2, ΔEΔt≥ħ/2, ħ=1.054e-34 Js
Derivation: Wave packet ΔxΔk≥½, p=ħk → ΔxΔp≥ħ/2. Formal (ΔA)²(ΔB)²≥|⟨[A,B]⟩|²/4 with [x,p]=iħ.
SIMULATION: Δx= 0.5nm → Δv≥115 km/s
