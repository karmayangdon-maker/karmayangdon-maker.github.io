5.1 Blackbody Radiation

A. Class Notes (Bullet Form):
  • Blackbody: Perfect absorber and emitter, absorptivity α=1
  • Classical equipartition gave Rayleigh-Jeans: \(u_\nu d\nu = \frac{8\pi\nu^2}{c^3}kT d\nu\)
  • Failed at high ν → Ultraviolet Catastrophe - energy → infinite
  • Planck's revolutionary idea (1900): Energy of oscillators quantized \(E_n=n h\nu\), \(h=6.626e-34 Js\)
  • Planck's law fits entire spectrum accurately
  • Two important laws derived from Planck:
    • Wien's Displacement: Hotter → λmax blue shift
    • Stefan-Boltzmann: Total power ∝ T⁴, explains stars luminosity
B. Homework Points:
  • Sun T~5800K → λmax~500nm green, but appears white due to broad curve
  • Human body T~310K → λmax~9.3 μm infrared, why thermal camera sees us
Working Formulas:
Planck: \(B_\lambda=\frac{2hc^2}{\lambda^5}\frac{1}{e^{hc/\lambda kT}-1}\)
Wien: \(\lambda_{max}T=2.898×10^{-3} mK\)
Stefan: \(j^*=σT^4, σ=5.67×10^{-8}\)
Full Derivation - Planck Law (Steps):
Step 1: Cavity modes density: No of modes per volume per freq \(g(ν)dν=8πν²/c³ dν\)
Step 2: Classical avg energy = kT → fails
Step 3: Quantized energy: Boltzmann factor \(P_n ∝ e^{-nhν/kT}\)
Step 4: Average energy \(\langle E\rangle = \frac{\sum n hν e^{-nhν/kT}}{\sum e^{-nhν/kT}} = \frac{hν}{e^{hν/kT}-1}\)
Step 5: Multiply g(ν) * ⟨E⟩ → Planck law. At low ν, \(e^{hν/kT}≈1+hν/kT\) gives Rayleigh-Jeans. At high ν gives Wien exponential cut-off.

Observation: As T increases, peak shifts left and area (total power) increases hugely

Solved Example: Find λmax for furnace 1500K.
Solution: λmax=2898μmK/T = 2898/1500=1.93 μm IR. Hence furnace glows red then white.

5.2 The Photoelectric Effect

Bullet Notes:
  • Discovered by Hertz 1887, explained by Einstein 1905 (Nobel 1921)
  • Experimental facts:
    • No emission if ν < ν₀ (threshold) even at high intensity
    • KE_max depends on frequency, NOT intensity
    • Instant emission (<10⁻⁹s) - wave theory predicts delay
    • Photo-current ∝ intensity
  • Einstein: Light consists of photons, each \(E=hν\)
  • Work function Φ = minimum energy to escape metal
  • For Bhutan: Solar panels work on same principle - photon knocks electron
\(K_{max}=hν-Φ\)
\(h=4.136×10^{-15} eV·s\)
Stopping potential: \(eV_0=K_{max}\)
Threshold: \(ν_0=Φ/h\)
Full Derivation:
Step 1: Photon absorbed fully by one electron
Step 2: Energy balance: Input = Output → \(hν = Φ + K_{max}\)
Step 3: If electron deeper, loses more in collisions → K < Kmax, so Kmax at surface
Step 4: Apply stopping voltage: Electron stopped when \(eV_0 = ½mv²_{max}=K_{max}\)
Step 5: Graph Kmax vs ν is straight line slope = h, intercept = -Φ. Millikan verified 1916 and measured h.

5.3 The Compton Effect

  • Arthur Compton 1923 scattered X-rays from graphite, observed λ increased
  • Classical Thomson scattering predicts no λ shift - fails
  • Explained if photon has momentum \(p=h/λ\) like particle
  • Only possible if photon collides like billiard ball with electron
  • Compton wavelength \(\lambda_c=h/m_ec=0.002426 nm\) is fundamental length scale of electron
  • Application: Compton telescope, medical imaging correction
\(\Delta\lambda = \frac{h}{m_ec}(1-\cosθ)\)
Max shift at 180°: \(2\lambda_c=0.00486 nm\)
Energy of scattered photon: \(E' = \frac{E}{1+\frac{E}{m_ec²}(1-\cosθ)}\)
Derivation (Energy+Momentum):
Step 1: Initial: photon \(E=hc/λ, p=h/λ\), electron at rest \(E_0=m_ec²\)
Step 2: Final: photon \(E'=hc/λ', p'=h/λ'\) at angle θ, electron recoils with \(p_e, E_e=√{(p_ec)²+(m_ec²)²}\)
Step 3: Conservation: \(p = p'cosθ + p_{ex}\), \(0=p'sinθ - p_{ey}\), \(E+m_ec² = E' + E_e\)
Step 4: Square and eliminate \(p_e\) → after algebra \( \frac{1}{E'}-\frac{1}{E}= \frac{1}{m_ec²}(1-\cosθ)\)
Step 5: Convert E=hc/λ → get Compton formula. Shows particle nature of light.
