5.1 Blackbody Radiation

Bullet Notes:
  • Blackbody perfect absorber α=1, small hole cavity
  • Stefan σT⁴ total power, Wien λmaxT=2.898e-3 mK peak shift
  • Rayleigh-Jeans 8πν²/c³ kT fails → UV catastrophe infinite energy
  • Planck E=nhν quantization solves, h=6.626e-34 Js
  • Sun 5800K λmax=500nm green, Human 310K 9.3μm IR

Derivation: Mode density g=8πν²/c³. Classical ⟨E⟩=kT → RJ divergent ∫. Planck Boltzmann average ⟨E⟩=hν/(e^{hν/kT}-1) → finite. Low ν→kT, high ν→exponential cutoff.

5.2 Photoelectric Effect

  • Hertz 1887, Einstein 1905 photon E=hν, Nobel
  • Laws: ν0 threshold, Kmax∝ν not intensity, instant <1ns, current ∝ intensity
  • Φ=work function, V0 stopping potential
  • Classical fails: should be time delay, Kmax should ∝ intensity

5.3 Compton Effect

  • Compton 1923 X-ray graphite λ increases with angle
  • Classical Thomson no shift, photon momentum needed
  • Δλ=λc(1-cosθ), λc=0.002426nm
  • Proves photon particle nature
DIAGRAM - Compton Collision:
Δλ=h/mec (1-cosθ), λc=0.002426nm, 180°→0.00486nm max
Derivation: Energy E+mec²=E'+Ee, momentum p=p'+pe vector. Eliminate pe → 1/E'-1/E=(1/mec²)(1-cosθ) → Δλ. Needs relativity Ee²=pe²c²+me²c⁴.

5.4 de Broglie Matter Wave

  • de Broglie 1924 λ=h/p, if light wave→particle then particle→wave
  • e- V=100V λ=0.123nm comparable to atom spacing → diffracts
  • Davisson-Germer 1927 proof, Bohr orbit 2πr=nλ
  • Electron microscope uses λ~0.004nm vs light 500nm → 100000× resolution
DIAGRAM - de Broglie Wavelength vs Voltage:
λ=h/p, e- λ(nm)=1.227/√V
Derivation: From photon λ=h/p hypothesized universal. Standing wave 2πr=nλ → L=nħ → En=-13.6/n²eV explains Bohr.
SIMULATION: V= 100V λ=0.1227nm atomic size!

5.7 Heisenberg Uncertainty

DIAGRAM - Uncertainty:
  • ΔxΔp≥ħ/2, ΔEΔt≥ħ/2, [x,p]=iħ
  • Gamma microscope: Δx~λ small → Δp~h/λ large
  • Consequences: zero-point E=ħ²/8mL², e- not in nucleus, tunneling, laser linewidth
ΔxΔp≥ħ/2, ħ=1.054e-34 Js
Derivation: Wave packet ΔxΔk≥½, p=ħk → ΔxΔp≥ħ/2. Formal Schwarz inequality ⟨ΔA²⟩⟨ΔB²⟩≥|⟨[A,B]⟩|²/4.
